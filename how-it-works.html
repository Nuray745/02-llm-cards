How an LLM Processes a Prompt

From the moment you press Enter to the first word of the response, here is what happens inside a large language model.

  1. 1

    User Input

    You type a prompt and submit it. The raw text is sent to the model.

  2. 2

    Tokenisation

    The tokenizer breaks text into smaller units called tokens.

  3. 3

    Embedding

    Tokens are converted into numerical vectors the model can understand.

  4. 4

    Attention Layers

    The model analyzes relationships between tokens and context.

  5. 5

    Prediction

    The model predicts the next most likely token in the sequence.

  6. 6

    Output Generation

    Predicted tokens are combined into the final response shown to the user.
